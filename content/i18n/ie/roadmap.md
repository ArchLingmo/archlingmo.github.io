## Plan de developation

Quo noi ancor va constructer. Quo es ja pret trova se in [Functionalitás](#recursos).
Li dates es un prevision e posse changear. Suggestiones e prioritás es discusset in nor
Discord, e chascun punctu deveni un apert pull request in GitHub.

Li prioritás veni de quo aparí max sovente in forums e in recensiones de altri distributiones:
li timore que un actualisation rupte li sistema, drivers, installar programmas sin terminal e
curar li computator sin esser un expert.

### 28/09 til 08/11 · Programmas sin complicationes (e secur)

- [x] **Butica de applicationes**: evaluationes del usatores e li permissiones de chascun application ante installar (li Butica con Flathub e li depositorias de Arch ja es ci)
- [x] **Trenar por installar**, quam sur un Mac: duplic-clicca un .exe, .deb, .rpm o AppImage e trena it a **Applicationes**
- [x] **Omnicos de exter functiona in un buxe isolat**: programmas de Windows (Bottles), paccages .deb/.rpm (Podman) e AppImages ne posse vider vor sistema o vor files sin permission
- [x] Un págine **Buxes** in li Parametres: vider, dar o remover accesse a files, rete, camera e microfon, e deleter un buxe
- [x] **Applicationes de Android** (APK) in un buxe, con Waydroid, visibil in li lansator quam quelcunc altri application
- [x] **Ludes con un clic**: Steam, Proton e modo de lude, con avise pri ludes con anti-cheat queles ne functiona in Linux
- [x] **Nativ ludes**: Zelda: Majora’s Mask (Zelda64Recomp / 2 Ship 2 Harkinian), Super Mario 64 (Ghostship), Star Fox 64 (Starship), Banjo-Kazooie (Banjo: Recompiled). Installa ex Butica → Nativ ludes. Al prim lansament, provide un ROM extraet de vor propri cartuche; li paccas ne contene dates del ludes.
- [x] **Ask about the screen** (Meta + S): circle an area to explain, translate or summarize it, with the AI on your computer
- [x] **App actions** (Lingmo Intents): apps offer actions to the Assistant, Flows and Spotlight
- [x] **Assistant with flows, agents and voice** (Meta + Shift + I)
- [x] **One keyboard and mouse for several computers** (Deskflow), with a shared clipboard

### 09/11 til 13/12 · Crear e partir

- [ ] **Un Lingmo Studio plu inteligent**: reencadrar sequente li subjecte principal (ne solmen li visage), saver qui parla in chascun parte (diarisation de parlatores) e effectes de audio sur chascun piste
- [ ] **Publicar li nativ ludes restant**: Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Ship of Harkinian) e Mario Kart 64 (SpaghettiKart), nu "Tost" in li Butica, tam rapid quam li licenties del projectes permisse distribuer les
- [ ] Finir li transfer del panelles lateral del Parametres, del Posta e del gerente de files a **LingmoUI**, con li sam aspecte quam li altri applicationes
- [ ] Preparar li via por li **session Wayland**: provar li applicationes e effectes de Lingmo in Wayland (li complet session es ancor previdet por 2027)

### 09/11 til 13/12 · Curar li computator

- [ ] **Monitor del sistema** (Ctrl + Shift + Esc): rete e carte grafic, in plu del processor, memorie e disco quel li Optimisator ja monstra
- [ ] **Information del sistema** con un buton "Copiar raport" por demandar auxilie in Discord o in forums
- [ ] Obscuration e funde del camera con un tallia de qualitá (capilles, auricules e spatules)

### 01/02 til 31/03/2027 · Hardware de chascun die

- [ ] **Printatores e scannatores** reconosset automaticmen, con un simplic ecran por adjunter les

### 01/02 – 31/05/2027 · Learning with Lingmo

- [ ] **Lingmo Tutor for children**: kids ask by speaking, typing, a photo of their notebook or a PDF of the lesson, and the Tutor explains in the system language, at their age level, with a clear voice and the text highlighted
- [ ] **Teach without handing over the answer**: hints and questions that get the child thinking, similar exercises to practise and maths checked before it is shown
- [ ] **Reading aloud with help**: the child reads a text and the Tutor helps with pronunciation and fluency
- [ ] **Parent mode**: age and subjects, screen time, history kept only on the computer and an always-on topic filter; nothing goes online
