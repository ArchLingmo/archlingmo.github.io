## Astaamaha

Waxyaabahan oo dhan maanta ayay ku jiraan ArchLingmo.

### Desktop-ka

- **Bar-ka sare** oo leh saacad, kalandar marka aad gujiso taariikhda, iyo astaamaha saxanka nidaamka
- **Xarunta kontaroolka**: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, habka mugdiga, iftiinka habeenka, ha i dhibin iyo codka, hal guddi gudihiisa
- **Dock** oo muujiya horudhac daaqadaha marka jiirka la dul saaro, oo leh asal **adag, muraayad ama asal la'aan**
- **Spotlight** (Super + Space): abka, faylasha, xisaab, beddelidda cabbirrada iyo lacagaha, iyo raadinta gudaha dukumentiyada
- **Taariikhda clipboard-ka** (Super + V)
- **Geesaha firfircoon**: jiirka u qaad gees si aad u aragto daaqadaha oo dhan, desktop-ka, ama u quful shaashadda
- **Saamaynno**: daaqado jelly oo kale u gariira, laambadda sixirka, iyo Alt+Tab oo u eg bogagga buug
- Menu guud oo xitaa loogu talagalay abka GTK
- **Widget-yada saacadda iyo cimilada** ee desktop-ka, oo leh saadaasha dhabta ah ee magaalada aad doorato, laguna ururiyay hal astaan oo ku jira bar-ka sare
- **Alt+Tab "Karuusel"**: ku beddel daaqadaha karuusel 3D ah, qaabka cover flow, oo leh milicsi
- **Astaamaha Lingmo**: mawduuc astaamo u gaar ah, oo leh astaamo siman oo qaab squircle ah (afargees geesaha la wareejiyay), laga dhisay WhiteSur. Astaamaha abka dhinac saddexaad si toos ah ayay u helaan isla saxankaas
- **Dock oo weyneysa** astaamaha ilaa 200% marka jiirka la dul saaro iyo **bar-ka sare** oo leh dhererro diyaarsan oo aad ka dooran karto

### Sirdoonka macmalka ah (kombiyuutarkaaga dushiisa)

- **Kaaliyaha** (Super + I): AI ku shaqeeya kombiyuutarkaaga, oo leh Ollama. Wuxuu akhriyaa dukumentiyadaada (PDF, DOCX…), qoraallada iyo kalandarka, wuxuu eegaa macluumaadka nidaamka, wuxuuna ku fuliyaa amarrada terminal-ka ee akhris-kaliya sanduuq go'doonsan. Oggolaanshahaaga ayuu ku sameeyaa qoraallo, dhacdooyin, hawlo iyo faylal: isbeddel kasta wuxuu weydiiyaa xaqiijin, waligiisna waxba ma tirtiro
- **AI-ga Spotlight**: ku bilow "ai:" si aad ula sheekaysato, ama qor su'aal ku dhammaata "?" oo dooro inaad AI-ga weydiiso. Wuxuu muujiyaa inta uu jawaab kastaa qaatay iyo xawaaraha ay ahayd, wuxuuna isticmaalaa qalabka uu Kaaliyuhu isticmaalo
- **Dejinta › Sirdoonka macmalka ah**: xaaladda Ollama, moodellada la rakibay, talooyin loogu talagalay kombiyuutarkan *gaarka ah*, soo dejinta moodellada oo leh horumarka, ikhtiyaarka "Ka fikir ka hor intaadan jawaabin", iyo oggolaanshaha iyo qalabka Kaaliyaha
- **Otomaatiyeeye**: hawl maalinle ah oo waqti go'an, oo leh amar iyo, haddii aad rabto, AI-ga maxalliga ah oo sharxaya natiijada
- **Qoraal-u-beddelka codka iyo qoraal-hoosaadyada** ee Dejinta › Sirdoonka macmalka ah: moodellada whisper.cpp oo leh calaamado iyo sumadda "Lagu taliyay kombiyuutarkaaga", oo ku xiga moodellada AI ee loogu talagalay qalabkaaga (llmfit)
- **Assistant flows**: build automations from blocks, a trigger (a time, a new e-mail, a voice command or a shortcut) plus app actions, follow every run step by step and approve once what the flow may do
- **Agents and voice**: assistants with their own instructions, model and tools, called in the chat, in a flow or from Spotlight ("ai:@name"). And talk to the Assistant by voice with Meta + Shift + I
- **Ask about the screen** (Meta + S): circle or select any area of the screen to explain, translate, summarize, copy its text or search the web. It is analyzed on your computer and nothing is sent
- **App actions** (Lingmo Intents): Clock, Mail, Notes, Files, Screenshots and other apps offer actions to the Assistant, Flows and Spotlight: type what you want, such as an alarm at 7, and confirm. In Settings › Artificial Intelligence you can turn off an app or a single action, and sending or changing anything always asks first

### Dejinta

- **Soo dhaweyn** marka ugu horreysa ee aad gasho: mawduuc iftiin ama mugdi, midabka muujinta, sawirka gidaarka iyo saamaynnada
- **Shaashadaha**: jiid si aad u habayso, wareeji oo dooro midda ugu muhiimsan
- **Iftiinka habeenka** oo leh jadwal toos ah, iyo beddelidda qaabka kiiboodhka ee bar-ka
- Bogagga **Gaaban-furayaasha** iyo **Bilowga** si aad u doorato waxa la furmaya marka nidaamku bilaabmo
- **Cusboonaysiin aan terminal lahayn**: ogeysiis ku jira bar-ka iyo badhan "Cusboonaysii"
- Waxaa lagu heli karaa **48 luqadood**
- **Folders that follow your language**: when you switch language, your personal folders (Documents, Videos, Pictures…) get the new names; folders that exist in two languages are merged without deleting anything, and you can undo it

### Kamarada iyo amniga

- **Qaabayn toos ah** oo kamarada ah inta lagu jiro kulamada, ab kasta (Meet, Zoom, Teams, Discord)
- **Akhriyaha faraha** si aad u gasho, u furto oo u oggolaato ereyada sirta ah ee maamulaha
- **Aqoonsiga wejiga** iyada oo loo marayo webcam-ka si aad u gasho oo u furto (haddii uu fashilmo, wuxuu weydiinayaa erayga sirta ah)

### Abka

- **Boostada**: akoonnada IMAP iyo POP3 oo si toos ah u aqoonsada bixiyaha, oo leh tilmaamo mid kasta (ereyada sirta ah ee abka ee Gmail, Yahoo iyo iCloud). Galal tusaya inta aan la akhrin, raadin iyo shaandhayn, xiddig, kaydin, dhaqaajinta iyo tirtiridda farriimo badan hal mar, xeerar toos ah, xiriirro leh dhammaystir toos ah iyo ogeysiisyada boosto cusub
- **Kalandarka iyo hawlaha** ee Boostada: muuqaalka bisha, toddobaadka, maalinta iyo ajandaha, dhacdooyin soo noqnoqda, xusuusin xitaa marka abka la xiro, iyo isku-waafajinta CalDAV (iCloud, Yahoo, Fastmail, Nextcloud). Hawlaha maanta, kuwa soo socda iyo kuwa dib u dhacay, iyo martiqaadyada kulamada (.ics) oo leh Aqbal, Laga yaabee ama Diid
- **Qoraallada** qaabka Notion: tafatire blog-yo ah, menu-ga "/", bogag hoose, astaamo, kuwa la jecel yahay, qashin-qub iyo raadin degdeg ah (Ctrl + K)
- **Hagaajiye**: guji CPU, Xusuusta ama Kaydinta si aad u aragto faahfaahinta: meesha booska diskigaagu aaday, gal-gal, oo leh talooyin, iyo abka ugu badan ee isticmaala xusuusta iyo processor-ka. Badhanka "Hagaaji oo Nadiifi" wuxuu nadiifiyaa kaydadka ku-meel-gaarka ah, kaydka cusboonaysiinta, diiwaannada duugga ah iyo runtime-yada Flatpak ee aan la isticmaalin
- Maareeyaha **faylasha** oo leh jiid-oo-dhig iyo kala-soocid sida Thunar oo kale
- **Terminal** leh mawduuca Dracula ee habka mugdiga iyo doorashada shell-ka (bash, zsh, fish…) ee dookhyada cusub, **tafatiraha qoraalka**, **xisaabiye** iyo **ciyaariyaha muuqaalka**
- **Saacadda iyo alaarmiyada**, **Sameeyaha Warbaahinta USB** si aad ISO ugu qorto USB, **Barnaamijyada Windows** (fur .exe oo arag nooca Linux ama beddel ku jira Dukaanka, ama ku tijaabi Bottles/Wine) iyo **Dukaanka barnaamijyada** oo leh Flathub iyo kaydadka Arch hal meel
- Abka oo dhan waxay wadaagaan muuqaalka Lingmo ee isku midka ah, mawduuc iftiin iyo mid mugdi ah
- **Sawirro iyo duubista shaashadda**, oo leh makarafoon iyo codka nidaamka
- Rakibaha **Calamares** iyo cod iyada oo loo marayo **PipeWire**
- **Ciyaaraha asalka ah**: Zelda: Majora’s Mask (Zelda64Recomp / 2 Ship 2 Harkinian), Super Mario 64 (Ghostship), Star Fox 64 (Starship), Banjo-Kazooie (Banjo: Recompiled). Ka rakib Dukaanka → Ciyaaraha asalka ah. Marka ugu horreysa ee aad furto, keen ROM laga soo saaray kartarijkaaga; xirmooyinku kuma jiraan xogta ciyaaraha.
- **Sawirka shaashadda** (Print, Shift + Print iyo Ctrl + Print): wax kasta oo Flameshot sameeyo (qalabka faallooyinka, ku dhejinta shaashadda, koobiyeynta, kaydinta iyo khadka amarrada) iyo wax ka badan: "Koobi qoraalka" oo leh OCR kombiyuutarkaaga dushiisa, qarinta xogta hal guji, xulasho caqli leh, sawir qaadis la rogrogo, habka bandhigga, taariikh la tafatiri karo iyo duubis GIF ah
- **Sawirrada**: sawirradaada oo ku jira maktabad, kuwa la jecel yahay iyo galal, oo leh isla sawirrada yaryar ee maareeyaha faylasha
- **Akhriyaha PDF**: sawirrada yaryar ee bogagga, tusmada, qoraallada iyo ku habaynta ballaca
- **Muuqaalka tiirarka ee Faylasha** (Ctrl + 3) iyo sawirro yaryar oo raacaya heerka freedesktop, oo la wadaago Sawirrada

### Muuqaal: Lingmo Studio

- **Lingmo Studio**: tafatire muuqaal oo lagu dhisay MLT, oo leh bogag la mid ah kuwa DaVinci Resolve: Tafatir, Midab, Saamaynno, Cod iyo Gudbin
- **Tafatirka**: jadwal-waqtiyeed leh luuq badan, ripple, roll, slip iyo slide, gooyniinka J iyo L, kilibyo isku dhafan, proxy-yo iyo guddi taariikheed
- **Midab**: giraangiraha midabka, qalooca, HSL, LUT-yada .cube iyo cabbirayaasha (scopes)
- **Cod**: isku-dare, isle'eeg (equalizer), cadaadiye (compressor), dhimista buuqa iyo is-waafajinta heerka codka ee LUFS
- **U dhoofi** MP4 si caadi ah, oo uu dardargeliyo kaarka sawirka (NVENC ama VA-API)
- **Samee qaybo gaaban**: wuxuu muuqaal dheer u beddelaa qaybo gaaban oo loogu talagalay Shorts, Reels, TikTok, Kwai, LinkedIn, X iyo Stories, oo leh dhererka iyo aagga badbaadada ah ee shabakad kastaa ku talisay, daqiiqadaha ugu fiican oo uu doortay AI maxalli ah (Ollama) iyo dib-u-qaabayn 9:16 ah oo raacaysa wejiga
- **Qoraal-hoosaadyo** la waafajiyay kelmad-kelmad iyadoo la adeegsanayo whisper.cpp, kombiyuutarkaaga dushiisa: sagaal qaab oo loogu talagalay akhriska (karaoke oo midabku dul marayo jumladda, kelmad la iftiimiyay, kelmad-kelmad, podcast, neon, kala-duwanaansho sare, laba qof oo hadlaya…) iyo u dhoofinta SRT, VTT iyo ASS
- **Cut**: the quick page for assembling a video, with the whole timeline in miniature above the detailed one, smart trim and shortcuts to Generate cuts and Captions
