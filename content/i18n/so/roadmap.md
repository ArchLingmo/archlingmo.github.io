## Khariidadda horumarinta

Waxa aan weli dhisi doonno. Waxa diyaar ah horeba waxaad ka helaysaa [Astaamaha](#recursos).
Taariikhuhu waa qiyaas, wayna isbeddeli karaan. Soo jeedinta iyo mudnaanta waxaa lagaga wada hadlaa
Discord-kayaga, shay kastana wuxuu noqdaa pull request furan oo ku jira GitHub.

Mudnaantu waxay ka timaaddaa waxa ugu badan ee laga hadlo goleyaasha iyo dib-u-eegista distro-yada kale:
cabsida in cusboonaysiin ay jebiso nidaamka, darawallada, rakibidda barnaamijyada iyadoo aan terminal la isticmaalin iyo
daryeelka kombiyuutarka iyadoo aan loo baahnayn aqoon farsamo.

### 28/09 ilaa 08/11 · Barnaamijyo aan dhib lahayn (oo ammaan ah)

- [x] **Dukaanka barnaamijyada**: qiimeynta isticmaalayaasha iyo oggolaanshaha ab kasta ka hor rakibidda (Dukaanka leh Flathub iyo kaydadka Arch horeba wuu jiraa)
- [x] **Jiid si aad u rakibto**, sida Mac-ga: laba-guji .exe, .deb, .rpm ama AppImage oo u jiid **Abka**
- [x] **Wax kasta oo dibadda ka yimaada wuxuu ku shaqeeyaa sanduuq go'doonsan**: barnaamijyada Windows (Bottles), xirmooyinka .deb/.rpm (Podman) iyo AppImage-yada ma arki karaan nidaamkaaga ama faylashaada oggolaansho la'aan
- [x] Bog **Sanduuqyada** ah oo ku jira Dejinta: arag, sii ama ka qaad gelitaanka faylasha, shabakadda, kamarada iyo makarafoonka, oo tirtir sanduuq
- [x] **Abka Android** (APK) oo ku jira sanduuq, Waydroid, oo ka muuqda furaha abka sida ab kasta oo kale
- [x] **Ciyaaro hal guji**: Steam, Proton iyo habka ciyaarta, oo leh digniin ku saabsan ciyaaraha anti-cheat-ka leh ee aan ku shaqeyn Linux
- [x] **Ciyaaraha asalka ah**: Zelda: Majora’s Mask (Zelda64Recomp / 2 Ship 2 Harkinian), Super Mario 64 (Ghostship), Star Fox 64 (Starship), Banjo-Kazooie (Banjo: Recompiled). Ka rakib Dukaanka → Ciyaaraha asalka ah. Marka ugu horreysa ee aad furto, keen ROM laga soo saaray kartarijkaaga; xirmooyinku kuma jiraan xogta ciyaaraha.

### 09/11 ilaa 13/12 · Abuur oo wadaag

- [ ] **Lingmo Studio ka caqli badan**: dib-u-qaabayn raacaysa mawduuca ugu muhiimsan (ma aha wejiga oo keliya), ogaanshaha cidda hadlaysa qayb kasta (kala-saarista hadlayaasha) iyo saamaynno cod oo luuq kasta ah
- [ ] **Daabac ciyaaraha asalka ah ee haray**: Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Ship of Harkinian) iyo Mario Kart 64 (SpaghettiKart), oo hadda ah "Dhowaan" Dukaanka, isla marka ay shatiyada mashaariicdu oggolaadaan in la qaybiyo
- [ ] U dhammaystir guurinta baararka dhinaca ee Dejinta, Boostada iyo maareeyaha faylasha ee **LingmoUI**, iyagoo yeelanaya muuqaal la mid ah abka kale

### 09/11 ilaa 13/12 · Daryeelka kombiyuutarka

- [ ] **Kormeeraha nidaamka** (Ctrl + Shift + Esc): shabakadda iyo kaarka sawirka, oo lagu daray processor-ka, xusuusta iyo diskiga uu Hagaajiyuhu horeba muujiyo
- [ ] **Macluumaadka nidaamka** oo leh badhanka "Koobi warbixinta" si aad caawimaad uga codsato Discord ama goleyaasha
- [ ] Qarinta iyo beddelidda asalka kamarada oo leh goyn tayo leh (timaha, dhegaha-dhegeysiga iyo garbaha)

### 14/12 ilaa 31/01/2027 · Cusboonaysii adigoon cabsanayn

- [ ] **Barta soo celinta tooska ah** ka hor cusboonaysiin kasta (btrfs), oo leh ikhtiyaar aad si toos ah ugu noqon karto liiska bilowga
- [ ] Badhanka **"Ku noqo shalay"** ee Dejinta iyo cusboonaysiiyaha

- [ ] **Maareeyaha darawallada**: wuxuu ogaadaa kaarka sawirka (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel) wuxuuna rakibaa darawalka saxda ah, isagoo la jaanqaadiya kernel-ka

### 01/02 ilaa 31/03/2027 · Qalabka maalin kasta

- [ ] **Daabacayaasha iyo iskaanarrada** si toos ah ayaa loo aqoonsadaa, oo leh shaashad fudud oo lagu daro

- [ ] **Batariga**: xadka dallacaadda 80% si uu u sii jiro muddo dheer, astaamaha tamarta iyo caafimaadka batariga
