**Helium** is the release on the download page today: the **2026.10.11** ISO. It's where
ArchLingmo stopped being "Lingmo running on Arch" and became a system with its own look
and its own apps.

## Why Helium

The **HELIUM** badge already sat on the Lingmo OS logo, on the About page in Settings. We
kept the name for the first release and turned the idea into a tradition: **every
ArchLingmo release is named after a chemical element**, picked for what it brings.

Helium is the second element of the table (atomic number **2**), light and noble: it
hardly reacts with anything. A good fit for a first release that, above all, had to be
light and stable.

## What came with Helium

### A whole desktop in Qt 6

- Top bar, dock, launcher, Spotlight and Control Center ported to **Qt 6, KDE Frameworks 6
  and KWin 6**, straight from the Arch repositories.
- A **dock** with window previews on hover and a solid, glass or no background.
- **Spotlight** (Super + Space) with apps, files, maths, unit and currency conversion.
- **Clipboard history** (Super + V), **hot corners** and effects such as wobbly windows,
  magic lamp and a book-page Alt+Tab.
- A global menu for GTK apps too, and clock and weather **widgets** on the desktop.

### Settings and first steps

- A **welcome** tour on first login: theme, accent colour, wallpaper and effects.
- **Displays** you drag into place, **night light**, **shortcuts** and **startup** pages,
  and **updates without a terminal**.
- **48 languages**.

### AI on your own computer

- The **Assistant** (Super + I), powered by Ollama: it reads your documents, notes and
  calendar, and only creates or changes something after asking for permission.
- **AI in Spotlight**, the **Settings › Artificial Intelligence** page with models
  recommended for your hardware, and the **Automator** for daily routines.

### Camera and security

- **Auto framing** and **background blur** for video calls, in any app.
- Log in and unlock with your **fingerprint** or your **face**.

### Apps

- **Mail** with calendar and tasks, Notion-style **Notes**, the **Optimizer**, **Clock and
  alarms**, the **USB Media Creator** and the **Terminal** with your choice of shell.
- The **Store**, with Flathub and the Arch repositories in one place, user ratings and each
  app's permissions before you install.

### Outside programs, without fear

- Double-click an **.exe, .deb, .rpm, AppImage or APK** and drag it to Applications.
- Everything from outside runs in an **isolated box** (Bottles, Podman, Waydroid), and the
  **Boxes** page shows what each one can reach.
- **One-click gaming** with Steam, Proton and GameMode, plus the **native games** in the Store.

### The ISO

A *live* image to try without installing, with the **Calamares** installer, sound through
**PipeWire** and tighter compression for a smaller download.
